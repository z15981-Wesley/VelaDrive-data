Vela Drive 支援

把 iPhone 變成電動車的行車儀表：大字時速、動力條、胎壓、3D 地圖導航、道路速限與測速提醒（依當地法規）、行程紀錄。

常見問題

怎麼連上車子？
設定 › 連接車輛，輸入 VIN 後按「開始配對」，到車上刷鑰匙卡並在車機確認。手機會以「唯讀」身分加入，只能讀取車況，不能開鎖或啟動車。

為什麼 GPS 時速和車上不一樣？
車上儀表通常刻意顯示得比實際快幾 %。連車行駛幾分鐘後，App 會自動學習你的車，之後沒連車時 GPS 時速也會接近車上的數字（設定 › 車輛參數）。

為什麼有些國家沒有測速照相提醒？
德國、瑞士、法國、比利時、盧森堡與土耳其不允許行車中使用測速照相提醒，App 會依所在位置自動關閉，只保留依道路速限的超速提醒。

導航語音在哪裡開？
預設關閉，以車機導航為主。導航時點畫面上的喇叭按鈕即可開關。

怎麼取消訂閱？
iPhone 設定 › Apple 帳號 › 訂閱項目 › Vela Pro。

回復購買
在 Pro 介紹頁點「回復購買」。家人共享群組的成員也能使用。

回報問題

請到 GitHub 問題回報 留言，附上 iPhone 型號、iOS 版本與 App 版本（設定 › 關於）。

Vela Drive Support

Turns your iPhone into an EV driving display: big speed, power bar, tyre pressures, 3D map navigation, road speed limits and speed alerts (where the law allows) and trip history.

FAQ

How do I connect to my car?
Settings › Connect car: enter the VIN, tap Start pairing, then tap your key card in the car and confirm on the screen. The phone is added as a read-only key: it can read the car’s status but cannot unlock or start it.

Why does GPS speed differ from the car?
Car speedometers deliberately read a few percent high. After a few minutes of driving connected, the app learns your car, so GPS speed without a connection is close to the car’s number too (Settings › Vehicle).

Why are there no speed-camera alerts in some countries?
Germany, Switzerland, France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Turkey don’t allow camera warnings while driving. The app switches them off there automatically and keeps speeding alerts based on road limits.

Spoken directions?
Off by default so you hear the car’s own navigation. While navigating, tap the speaker button on screen.

Cancel a subscription
iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Vela Pro.

Restore purchases
Tap Restore on the Pro screen. Family Sharing members can use Pro too.

Report a problem

Open an issue at GitHub with your iPhone model, iOS version and app version (Settings › About).
