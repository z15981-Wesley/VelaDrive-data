Vela Drive 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

Vela Drive 不需要註冊帳號，開發者不蒐集、不儲存、也不出售你的個人資料。App 內沒有廣告、追蹤或分析工具。

留在你手機上的資料

  • 設定、行程紀錄（路線、距離、時間、耗電）、0–100 計時紀錄、離線圖資：只存在這支 iPhone，可在 App 內刪除，刪除 App 即全部移除。
  • 車輛藍牙鑰匙：存在 iPhone 鑰匙圈（僅此裝置），只用來以唯讀身分和你自己的車連線。
  • 歌詞暫存：查到的歌詞存在手機的快取資料夾，方便下次直接顯示。

App 會連線的服務

為了提供功能，App 會直接向下列服務送出必要的請求，開發者看不到這些內容：

  • Apple 地圖與 Apple 天氣（WeatherKit）：顯示地圖、規劃路線、查詢目前天氣時，會把位置送給 Apple。適用 Apple 隱私權政策。
  • OpenStreetMap（Overpass API）：查詢附近的道路速限與測速點時，送出所在區域的座標。
  • 政府資料開放平臺（內政部警政署）：下載台灣測速執法設置點，不含個人資料。
  • GitHub：下載離線圖資檔。
  • 你自行設定的歌詞資料庫、Musixmatch（使用你自己的金鑰）、iTunes 搜尋：查詢歌詞、歌曲原名與封面時，送出歌名、歌手與歌曲長度。

權限

  • 定位：顯示 GPS 時速、地圖、導航與測速提醒；行車中可在背景繼續運作。
  • 藍牙：直接連接你的車，讀取時速、功率與胎壓（唯讀，無法開鎖或啟動車輛）。
  • Apple Music：讀取正在播放的歌曲以顯示同步歌詞與播放控制。
  • 動作與健身：用手機感測器計算 G 值。
  • 通知：離車提醒（車門未關、未上鎖、胎壓異常）。

購買

訂閱與購買都由 Apple 的 App Store 處理，開發者不會取得你的付款資料。

兒童

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童，也不會蒐集兒童的個人資料。

聯絡

有任何問題，請到 GitHub 問題回報 留言。

Vela Drive Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Vela Drive needs no account. The developer does not collect, store or sell your personal data. There are no ads, trackers or analytics in the app.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Settings, trip history (route, distance, time, energy), 0–100 results and offline map packs are stored only on this iPhone. You can delete them in the app; deleting the app removes everything.
  • The car’s Bluetooth key is kept in the iPhone Keychain (this device only) and is used only to connect to your own car as a read-only key.
  • Lyrics that were found are cached on the phone so they show straight away next time.

Services the app contacts

To work, the app sends the requests it needs directly to these services; the developer cannot see them:

  • Apple Maps and Apple Weather (WeatherKit): your location is sent to Apple for maps, routes and current weather. The Apple Privacy Policy applies.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API): the coordinates of the area around you, to look up road speed limits and speed cameras.
  • Taiwan government open data (National Police Agency): the list of speed cameras; no personal data is sent.
  • GitHub: offline map pack downloads.
  • The lyrics database you set up, Musixmatch (with your own key) and iTunes Search: song title, artist and length, to find lyrics, original titles and artwork.

Permissions

  • Location: GPS speed, map, navigation and speed alerts; it can keep running in the background while you drive.
  • Bluetooth: a direct, read-only connection to your car for speed, power and tyre pressure (it cannot unlock or start the car).
  • Apple Music: the playing song, for synced lyrics and playback controls.
  • Motion: g-forces from the phone’s sensors.
  • Notifications: leaving-the-car reminders (open door, unlocked, tyre pressure).

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store; the developer never receives your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from children.
